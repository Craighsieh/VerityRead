## English (`en`)

Short description:

> Local AI reading assistant for accurate summaries, page questions, and translation—with page content kept on your device.

Detailed description:

> VerityRead is a local AI reading assistant for Chrome. Summarize the current page, identify key points, ask grounded questions, explain selected text, and create bilingual translations without sending page content to a cloud inference service.
>
> Choose Chrome Built-in AI or your own Ollama model at `127.0.0.1:11434`. The active Provider is always visible and VerityRead never silently switches Providers.
>
> Privacy is part of the product:
>
> - The current tab is read only after you start a page action.
> - Page content, prompts, and AI replies are processed on your device.
> - Summary caching and local history are off by default.
> - Persistent website access is optional and granted one exact site at a time.
> - A privacy receipt explains the Provider, inference location, and storage behavior for each task.
>
> Model or language-pack downloads and Chrome extension updates may use the network, but page content is not included in those requests. VerityRead has no account, analytics, advertising, or developer-operated inference server.
